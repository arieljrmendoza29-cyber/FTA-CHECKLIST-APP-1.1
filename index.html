Install the app

Add it to your home screen.

It takes about 10 seconds. After that it opens like any app, full screen, and works without internet.

Your device

iPhone or iPad

  1. Open the checklist in Safari
  2. Tap Share (the square with an arrow)
  3. Tap Add to Home Screen, then Add
Your device

Android

  1. Open the checklist in Chrome
  2. Tap Install app in the pop-up
  3. Tap Install
Your device

Windows or Mac

  1. Open the checklist in Chrome or Edge
  2. Click Install app in the pop-up
  3. Click Install
